First Structured Chaos Community Ecosystem

It is both a community and an ecosystem it's made out of multiple communities, each with its own leadership and rules. All communities work together and follow the same principles.

Feel free to get inspired by the FISH ＜＞＜ COMMUNITY. But don't treat it as the only way of doing things. You can structure things differently.

COMMUNITY PRINCIPLES

  1. FUN - Don't forget to have fun
  2. COMMON SENSE - Not everything needs a rule, sometimes the best guide is common sense
  3. STRUCTURED CHAOS - Creativity often comes from chaos. Embrace it, but don't let it control you
  4. CONFLICT AND CREATIVITY - It's okay to disagree. Conflict can lead to better ideas and innovations
  5. COMMUNICATION - Resolving conflicts requires respectful communication. Address problems early before they escalate
  6. INNOVATION - Always look for better ways to do things. Innovation is the key to progress
  1. HUMILITY - Don't let your ego get in the way. Everyone has something to teach you
  2. SIMPLICITY - The simplest solution is often the best one. Don't overcomplicate things
  3. DECENCY - Don't be a degenerate. Treat others with respect and kindness
  4. OPEN-MINDEDNESS - Be open to new ideas and experiences. They're the best way to learn and grow
  5. AUTHENTICITY - Don't keep pretending to be someone you're not, it's a waste of energy
  6. SUCCESS AND CREATIVITY - Success and money are important, but they shouldn't limit your creativity. Use them as tools for growth, not as the final goal.

Human nature is complex. People are different, and not everyone will understand your principles or rules just because they are told to. Good and evil exist, and everyone comes from different cultures with different perspectives. Some people don't speak English or know how to communicate, and some people don't have the mental capacity or knowledge to understand complicated things.

It's important to remember that things will never be perfect if humans are involved, so it's important to keep a balance between everything.
